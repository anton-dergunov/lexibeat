**Hard to say.** For every word, set `hard_to_say` to true only when learners **commonly say it
wrong**: a spelling that suggests the wrong sound, or a stress where a {{learner_language}} speaker
would not put it. A word that is merely long, has many syllables or is a tongue-twister, but that
people do say correctly, is **not** hard to say. Most words are not; in a language written as it is
said, almost none are.
