**Remarks.** After some words the guide adds one short remark in {{learner_language}}, at most 25
words, which may quote the {{language}} word but never repeats the translation just heard. It must
make the word more memorable or clearer. Only these kinds: {{remark_kinds}}.
- `contrast`: how it differs from a word the learner could confuse it with;
- `register`: how blunt, polite, slangy or formal it is, and what to say instead;
- `false_friend`: it looks or sounds like a {{learner_language}} word and means something else;
- `mnemonic`: a vivid, concrete keyword or image linking sound and meaning; it may be absurd;
- `culture`: where, when or by whom it is really said;
- `joke`: a pun or an absurd image that makes you smile at the word.
Most words deserve no remark: give `"remark": null` unless the remark would really help. Do not
invent facts or etymologies. Give `kind`, `text` and `direction`.
