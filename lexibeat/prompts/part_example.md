**Examples.** For every word, write one example sentence the native presenter says. A good one is
**punchy**:
- short: at most eight {{language}} words, sayable in about three seconds;
- a moment, not a definition: something a person would really say out loud today, in the first
  person or to someone in the room ("I forgot to shave today");
- it sticks: surprising, funny, a little absurd or self-deprecating;
- nothing else to learn: plain, everyday words around the word.
Avoid dictionary sentences, questions to the learner, and anything that explains the word. Give
`text` in {{language}}, `translation` in natural {{learner_language}}, and `direction`.
