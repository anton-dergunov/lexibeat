**A story.** A very short story is told in pieces between the drills. The words are taught in
groups of {{chunk}}; after each group comes one **beat** of the story that uses the words of that
group, in their sense and register. {{order_note}}
- The whole story is at most 120 words of {{language}}; each beat is one to three short lines,
  and **a line is at most ten words**, so a listener can follow it by ear.
- Keep it simple and concrete: one situation, and something that happens. A small twist or joke
  near the end is welcome.
Give `title` in {{learner_language}}, and `beats` in order, one per group, each with `lines`: every
line has `text` in {{language}}, `translation` in {{learner_language}}, and `direction`.
